# Flying and Tracing

Agricultural landscapes of Guatacondo and Pampa Iluga.

**Hugo Romero-Toledo**

A 70-second animated flyover over satellite imagery, with progressive tracing of existing mapping outputs. English labels and instrumental music. The sequence covers Guatacondo 1, Guatacondo 2, and light and dark ground in Pampa Iluga.

## Video copies

- [Full HD video](assets/flying-and-tracing-hd.mp4): 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 22.3 MB.
- [Mobile video](assets/flying-and-tracing-mobile.mp4): 960 × 540, 30 fps, 8.8 MB.

## Interpretation and credits

This is an animated view of satellite imagery, not recorded drone footage. Dashed lines indicate uncertain or fragmentary traces. The curved feature in Iluga remains open as mapped. Guatacondo 1 corresponds to the mapping source formerly labelled G5.

Mapping and development: Hugo Romero-Toledo. Independently funded. Reference imagery: Google Satellite, credited in the video. Original source data and the full-quality master are retained separately.
